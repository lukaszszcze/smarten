# SmartEn

A practice platform for two English competitions for Polish primary-school
students: the Mazowieckie kuratoryjny competition and OJAJ.

## Language

### OJAJ

**OJAJ**:
Olimpiada Jezyka Angielskiego dla Juniorow, the national English olympiad run by
WSJO in Poznan, marked on its own scale and kept apart from kuratoryjny results.
_Avoid_: the olympiad (ambiguous with other WSJO olympiads)

**Etap I, czesc II**:
The second part of the OJAJ school stage: an online test written at school, 45
questions at one point each in 45 minutes, generated individually for each
student from a pool of tasks.
_Avoid_: online round, part 2 (without the stage)

**Paper**:
A timed test converted from an OJAJ paper published at ojaj.wsjo.pl.
_Avoid_: vault test

**Online test**:
A timed 45-item test written for this app in the format of etap I, czesc II:
10 word formation, 10 wisielec, 10 key word transformations, 10 grammar and
vocabulary multiple choice and 5 UK culture multiple choice.
_Avoid_: paper, mock

**Vault**:
The fixed set of online tests that a draw chooses from.
_Avoid_: pool (in this app a pool is where best scores are kept), bank

**Draw**:
Picking an online test at random from the vault, among the unseen ones; once
every test is done, among those done least often.

**Unseen**:
An online test with no submitted attempt. A test opened and abandoned is still
unseen.

**Own items**:
Items written for this app rather than taken from an OJAJ paper; their titles
say so ("zadania wlasne").
_Avoid_: generated items, fake items
