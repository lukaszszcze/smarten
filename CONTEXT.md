# SmartEn

A practice platform for two English competitions for Polish primary-school
students: the Mazowieckie kuratoryjny competition and OJAJ.

## Language

### OJAJ

**OJAJ**:
Olimpiada Jezyka Angielskiego dla Juniorow, the national English olympiad run by
WSJO in Poznan, marked on its own scale and kept apart from kuratoryjny results.
_Avoid_: the olympiad (ambiguous with other WSJO olympiads)

**Etap I, czesc II**:
The second part of the OJAJ school stage: an online test written at school, 45
questions at one point each in 45 minutes, generated individually for each
student from a pool of tasks.
_Avoid_: online round, part 2 (without the stage)

**Paper**:
A timed test converted from an OJAJ paper published at ojaj.wsjo.pl.
_Avoid_: vault test

**Online test**:
A timed 45-item test in the format of etap I, czesc II, put together from the
vault at each draw: 10 word formation, 10 wisielec, 10 key word
transformations, 10 grammar and vocabulary multiple choice and 5 UK culture
multiple choice.
_Avoid_: paper, mock

**Vault**:
The store of own items that every online test is put together from, written in
batches. Since 2026-10-25; before that it was a fixed set of three tests, whose
items are now its first three batches.
_Avoid_: pool (in this app a pool is where best scores are kept), bank

**Batch**:
45 own items written, checked and approved together, in the shape of one
online test. An item's vault id is its batch and question number, "02-14".

**Draw**:
Putting an online test together from the vault for one pupil, aimed at his
mistakes: per task a few repeats, the rest unseen items, then the least seen.

**Repeat**:
An item in a drawn test because of an earlier mistake: either the same item,
missed last time it was asked, or an unseen item of a mistake type he misses.
The review marks every item he has seen before "powtorka".

**Mistake type**:
The one point that makes an item hard (-ance/-ence, double letters,
conditionals, a branch of the UK mind map), from a closed list shared across
tasks. A miss counts for the item's type, except a typed miss in word
formation or wisielec, which takes its type from the answer.

**Unseen**:
An item that was in no submitted online test. An item in a test opened and
abandoned is still unseen.

**Own items**:
Items written for this app rather than taken from an OJAJ paper; their titles
say so ("zadania wlasne").
_Avoid_: generated items, fake items
