Word formation and wisielec

Double letters

You cannot hear a double letter: forgetable and forgettable sound the same. Two rules decide most words: when the last letter of a word doubles before an ending, and what happens when a prefix or an ending meets the same letter. The rest have to be learnt by heart.

Common mistakes

Common mistakeunforgetable
Rightunforgettable

for-GET is stressed at the end, so the t doubles before -able.

Common mistakeaccidentaly
Rightaccidentally

accidental + ly: both l's stay.

Common mistakeaccomodation
Rightaccommodation

Two c's and two m's: a-cc-o-mm-o-dation.

Common mistakebegining
Rightbeginning

be-GIN is stressed at the end, so the n doubles.

Common mistakeneccessary
Rightnecessary

One c, two s's: like a shirt with one collar and two sleeves.

When a prefix ends with the letter the word starts with, keep both: un + necessary = unnecessary. The same goes for an ending: sudden + ness = suddenness, real + ly = really.

Watch out

  • Stress decides: pre-FER → preferred, but OF-fer → offered.
  • Endings that start with a consonant never double anything: forgetful, equipment, commitment.
  • British spelling doubles l: travelled, cancelled, jeweller. American English writes traveled.
  • all- at the front of a word has one l: already, always, almost, altogether. But all right is two words.
  • until has one l, but till has two.
