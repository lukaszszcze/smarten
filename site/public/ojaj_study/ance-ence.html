Word formation and wisielec

-ance or -ence?

-ance and -ence sound exactly the same, and so do -ant and -ent. You cannot hear the difference, but a word from the same family usually tells you which one it is. Where nothing helps, the lists below are the words to learn.

The rule

The letter to watch is in green.

1. The -ant or -ent word tells you

-ant becomes -ance

  • abundant→abundance
  • arrogant→arrogance
  • brilliant→brilliance
  • distant→distance
  • dominant→dominance
  • elegant→elegance
  • extravagant→extravagance
  • fragrant→fragrance
  • ignorant→ignorance
  • important→importance
  • radiant→radiance
  • relevant→relevance
  • reluctant→reluctance
  • significant→significance
  • tolerant→tolerance

-ent becomes -ence

  • absent→absence
  • adolescent→adolescence
  • competent→competence
  • confident→confidence
  • convenient→convenience
  • different→difference
  • evident→evidence
  • excellent→excellence
  • independent→independence
  • innocent→innocence
  • intelligent→intelligence
  • magnificent→magnificence
  • obedient→obedience
  • patient→patience
  • permanent→permanence
  • present→presence
  • prominent→prominence
  • silent→silence
  • violent→violence

-ent becomes -ency

  • consistent→consistency
  • decent→decency
  • efficient→efficiency
  • fluent→fluency
  • frequent→frequency
  • urgent→urgency

If you know the adjective, you know the noun: important → importance, different → difference. The letter stays the same in the whole family. And listen to the c or g before the ending: a hard one (k, g) goes with -ance (significance, elegance), a soft one (s, j) with -ence (innocence, intelligence).

Most verbs take -ance: perform → performance. Verbs that end in a stressed -er or -ur (pre-FER, oc-CUR) or in -ere take -ence, and so do exist, insist and persist. The other -ence verbs below have to be learnt.

Watch out

  • assist and resist take -ance (assistance, resistance), but exist, insist and persist take -ence (existence, insistence, persistence).
  • prefer → preference and refer → reference: the stress moves to the front, so the r does not double. occur → occurrence keeps the stress, so it does.
  • independent never has an a: independence, independently.
  • In British spelling licence is the noun and license the verb; practice and practise work the same way.
  • maintain → maintenance and hinder → hindrance also change inside: see When the base word changes.

Check yourself

Type the word made from the word in capitals, then press Check or Enter. The spelling has to be exact, as on the test.

  1. I don't see the (RELEVANT) of this question to our topic.

  2. Do you believe in the (EXIST) of ghosts?

  3. The actors were cheered at the end of their (PERFORM).

  4. Thank you for your (ASSIST) with the heavy bags.

  5. My aunt's (INSIST) on paying for everyone was very kind.

  6. There is a big (DIFFER) between the two plans.

  7. Running a marathon needs a lot of (ENDURE).

  8. My (PREFER) is for a seat by the window.
