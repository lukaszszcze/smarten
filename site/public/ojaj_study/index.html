Study pages

One page for each kind of mistake in word formation and wisielec in the online tests so far. Each page shows the mistake, the rule behind it and the words to learn, and ends with a short check.

How to use these pages
  1. Read one page a day. Say the words in the lists aloud and spell them.
  2. Do "Check yourself" without looking back up the page.
  3. Tick "I've learnt this" when you get everything right, then practise in SmartEn.
